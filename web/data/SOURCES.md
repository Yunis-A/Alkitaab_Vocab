# Data sources

- `source/arabic-vocab.txt`: the vocabulary list (Alif Baa to Al-Kitaab part 3), supplied by the project owner.
- `conjugations.json`: full conjugation tables of weak, hamzated, doubled and quadriliteral verbs, generated by the
  Arabic conjugation module on **English Wiktionary** (`scripts/fetch-conjugations.mjs`). Wiktionary text and
  data are available under CC BY-SA 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/ . Re-used here with
  attribution to Wiktionary contributors; the forms are stored on each word in `src/data/words.json`.
- `root-check.json`, `root-check.csv`: results of comparing each word's root with the root on its Wiktionary entry
  (`scripts/verify-roots.mjs`). Differences are listed, never applied automatically.
- `root-decisions.json`: roots settled by hand, with the evidence (Wiktionary, Lane's Lexicon, Lisan al-Arab).
- `past-forms.json`: dictionary forms for verb rows whose list entry gives only a present or inflected form.
- `enrichment.json`: roots, parts of speech, genders and verb forms from the review pass.
- `review.csv`: every row that was inferred, corrected or flagged, for checking by hand.

`cache/` (raw Wiktionary responses) is git-ignored and can be deleted; the scripts rebuild it.
